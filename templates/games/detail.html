What do the move types mean?
  • !! Brilliant: a good piece sacrifice, played when you were not already winning easily.
  • ! Great: the only good move; every other move would have been at least a mistake.
  • ★ Best: Stockfish's top move.
  • 👍 Excellent: almost as good as the best move (loses at most 2% winning chance).
  • ✓ Good: a little worse than the best move (loses at most 5%).
  • ✗ Miss: your opponent had just made a mistake and you did not punish it. It still counts in the inaccuracy / mistake / blunder numbers above.
  • ?! Inaccuracy, ? Mistake, ?? Blunder: lose winning chances, using the same rules as Lichess.

Based on Chess.com's published rules; their exact model is not public, so labels can differ a little. Accuracy is calculated with Lichess's open-source formula.
